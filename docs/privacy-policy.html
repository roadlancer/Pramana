Pramana Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Pramana is a Chrome extension for taking screenshots and screen recordings and keeping them in your own Google Drive. Pramana has no servers of its own. Everything you capture goes directly from your browser to your Google Drive, and nobody else, including the developers of Pramana, can see it.

What Pramana accesses, and why

DataWhyWhere it goes
Your Google account name and email Shown in the Pramana popup so you know which account you're using Stays in your browser
Google sign-in token Lets Pramana save to and read from your Drive Stored in your browser; sent only to Google
Screenshots and recordings you make The purpose of the extension Your Google Drive, in a folder called “Pramana”. A finished recording is kept in your browser until you save or discard it
The contents of a tab or your screen Only while you take a screenshot or record, after you click a Pramana button (and, for your screen, choose what to share in Chrome) Only into the screenshot or recording above
Your microphone Only if you turn on “Include microphone”, and only while recording Only into the recording
Your settings (microphone on/off, video format) Remembering your choices Stays in your browser

Google Drive access

Pramana asks Google only for the drive.file permission. This lets Pramana see and change only the files Pramana itself created. It cannot see any other file in your Drive. You can remove Pramana's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Files are shared with others only when you click “Share link” on a file. Then anyone with the link can view that file, until you click “Stop sharing”.

What Pramana does not do

  • No servers, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising.
  • No data is sold, rented or given to anyone.
  • Nothing is captured in the background: capture starts only when you click a Pramana button.
  • Pramana does not read the pages you visit. It runs on a page only when you choose “Select Area” there.

Pramana's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

Delete files in the Pramana Library or directly in Google Drive. Signing out of Pramana removes the sign-in token from your browser; uninstalling the extension removes everything it stored in your browser.
