Pramana

Screenshots and screen recordings, saved to your own Google Drive. A free Chrome extension.

Screenshots

  • Capture the visible tab in one click, or drag to select an area
  • Annotate with arrows, boxes, text, highlights and emoji; blur passwords and personal details
  • Copy the image to paste anywhere, or save it to Google Drive

Screen recording

  • Record a tab or your whole screen, with your microphone if you like
  • Pause, resume and stop from the toolbar icon or with keyboard shortcuts
  • Review before saving; MP4 by default

Your files stay yours

  • Everything is saved to a “Pramana” folder in your own Google Drive
  • Pramana can only see the files it created (Google's drive.file permission)
  • No Pramana servers, no analytics, no ads
